# Titt's Questionably Prepared Encounter Generator

A free, quick D&D encounter generator for Game Masters whose players have gone somewhere they absolutely did not prepare for.

Pick a **Tier of Play**, generate an encounter, and let Titt provide the enemy, attitude, motive, terrain, combat behaviour, complications and loot while you pretend this was the plan all along.

## What it Generates

The generator currently includes:

- Enemy
- Attitude
- How Many?
- What Are They Doing?
- What Do They Want?
- Motive
- First Dialogue Line
- Terrain
- Combat Style
- Modifications
- Complication
- Reward / Loot

Each category can be rerolled individually, locked, or cleared.

## Tier of Play

The selected Tier of Play controls which enemy pool is used.

- **Newbies** – For very low-level parties and newer players
- **Tier 1** – Local threats and early adventuring
- **Tier 2** – Established adventurers and more dangerous monsters
- **Tier 3** – Major threats, powerful monsters and high-level enemies
- **Tier 4** – Legendary threats and world-shaking encounters
- **PUNISH THEM** – Balance has left the building

The generator is designed as an improvisation aid rather than a strict encounter-balancing calculator. GMs should still adjust enemy numbers and statblocks to suit their group.

## How to Use It

1. Choose a Tier of Play.
2. Click **Generate Encounter**.
3. Lock any results you want to keep.
4. Click a category name to reroll only that category.
5. Clear individual results if you do not need them.
6. Use **Copy Encounter** to copy the generated encounter into your notes.
